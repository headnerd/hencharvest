# CONTENT BIBLE

**Read this to judge the voice. If these don't make you exhale through your nose, we
don't have a game yet and the systems don't matter.**

---

## 1. THE DARK LORD

He is the funniest character in the game and he is almost entirely in memos. He is never
threatening. He is *disappointed*. He has never been observed raising his voice. He has,
on one occasion, sent a calendar invite, and that was the most frightening thing anyone in
the fortress can remember.

### The pit trap memo — the thesis statement of the game

> **From:** The Dark Lord
> **Re:** Pit trap (again)
>
> I want to be unambiguous. I do not care whether the pit trap is used. I care that it is
> documented. The last inspection was an embarrassment for all of us.
>
> Please do not make me file a follow-up.

### More memos

> **Re:** Q3 targets
> I am aware these are the same targets as Q2. I am aware of why. Please stop explaining
> the variance to me and put it in the deck.

> **Re:** Urgent
> Nothing in this memo is urgent. I have titled it urgent because the previous one was
> also urgent and I would like to see how the two compare.

> **Re:** The chosen one
> Noted.

That is his entire contribution to the Chosen One ticket. Forty-six comments by you,
one *noted* by him. It is the funniest single data point in the game and it costs four
words.

> **Re:** Your transfer
> I have read your request. I have not decided. I want you to know that I have not decided
> in a way that is not about you.

## 2. THE CHOSEN ONE

He is a workload. He is a recurring appointment. He is never mysterious.

He is described entirely in the language of intake paperwork, and that register never
slips.

> The Chosen One has entered the premises. He was carrying a +3 longsword and a grievance.
> I have logged both. The sword is in Lost & Found. The grievance is with Legal.

> Subject: Re: The Chosen One — status of the longbow
> He has upgraded. This is not speculation; the longbow is documented in three separate
> incident reports filed in the same week by three different people.
>
> Deprecation notices were available. I did not issue one. I want that on the record.

**The recurring line.** He speaks once or twice per run, never in the same words twice,
and never as wit — he is sincere, and he is always slightly wrong about the fortress.

> "I'm not here to kill you. I'm here for the thing at the bottom."
>
> The thing at the bottom was a filing cabinet. This was his third visit. We have a
> continuity problem.

**On the Chosen One never dying:** he cannot be permanently removed. He is a *condition*.
This is the joke: you cannot deprecate destiny, and if you try, the paperwork increases.

## 2b. DEATH, REPORTED (locked decision 1)

The player never sees a death. They see the *paperwork of* a death, and the paperwork is
always the same length regardless of what happened. The horror is the flatness.

**Template — the death referral, appears whenever the pit trap is used:**

> **INCIDENT REFERRAL — FORM 7B**
> Re: The chosen one / or, on occasion, a party of four (kobold, medium, two unexplained)
>
> Status: Requisitioned.
> Location: The pit trap.
> Witness: You.
> Follow-up required: No. There is no follow-up. The trap is compliant.
>
> **Notes:** The trap performed as designed. The paperwork was filed before the incident
> and remains valid after it. No further action is available to any party.

**The "No. There is no follow-up." is the whole game in two sentences.** The trap is
compliant. Someone is dead. These facts are in the same document and do not interact.

**On the unexplained kobolds:** small, recurring, unexplained. They arrive in fours. They
are not in any system. Nobody has ever successfully filed them and the referral form has a
line for "number of additional parties" which is the only line on the form that gets used.

> *Goblin 4:* Were you going to log the kobolds separately?
> *Goblin 1:* There's no line for them.
> *Goblin 4:* There's a line for "additional parties."
> *Goblin 1:* Then they'll be the additional parties.
> *Goblin 4:* They are going to read that form and they are going to count themselves.

## 3. THE GOBLINS

They do the actual work. They are the only ones who tell you the truth. Their morale is
your information economy, so their scenes carry the most mechanical weight in the game.

**Tone:** they are competent and exhausted. This is a workplace, not a villain's lair. The
comedy is that they have *processes*, given that they are goblins.

### Standup, Tuesday — the recurring bit

> *Goblin 4:* I fixed the bug.
> *Goblin 1:* That was not in the sprint.
> *Goblin 4:* It was a small bug.
> *Goblin 1:* Everything is small until it is not.

### More standup

> *Goblin 2:* Who ate the requisition forms?
> *Goblin 4:* That is not a question anyone here is going to answer.
> *Goblin 2:* I am going to file it as a security concern.
> *Goblin 4:* You are going to file it and I am going to correct it. Those are different
> activities and they are not going well for either of us.

> *Goblin 1:* The Dark Lord walked past today.
> *Goblin 4:* In what direction?
> *Goblin 1:* Away.
> *Goblin 4:* Then it is fine. Everything is fine. Carry on.

### The union beat (this is the escalation)

At low morale the standup becomes organized, and it is played *deadpan*, not as menace.

> *Goblin 1:* We are not asking for better conditions.
> *Goblin 4:* We are asking to be informed when he is upset.
> *Goblin 1:* That is a small ask for a building that is on fire.
> *Goblin 4:* It is a very small ask for a building that is *often* on fire.

## 4. THE DRAGON

He is your direct report in all the ways that matter and a liability in all the ways that
get reported. **He eats the paperwork**, which turns every review cycle into a retrieval
puzzle. He is doing his best. That is the joke.

### The review, fourth rewrite

> **Strengths:** thermoregulation, initiative, general intimidation of the mailroom.
> **Needs improvement:** closing the incubator.
>
> Also I am told my previous review was eaten. I am aware this is my fourth draft. I have
> started keeping copies. The copies are also food. I am working on it.

### One line from the dragon that should be replayed a lot

> "I want to be better. I want to be better and I do not know what I am doing wrong and I
> have thought about it every day for a year."

There is no punchline after that. Do not add one. The absence is the joke, and it is the
one moment in the game where a character is genuinely sad. It is also the only moment
where deadpan is *the correct choice* rather than the funniest one.

## 5. THE WORKPLACE TEXTURE

The background is a hell dimension operated like a mid-size firm. Deadpan comes from the
gap between the two, and the gap must be *consistent*.

> The infernal supply chain is a single room, one clerk, and a door that is also a
> demon. The demon has a badge. The badge works. This has been fine for eight hundred
> years and no one has examined it.

> Health & Safety notice, posted 400 years ago and never taken down:
> "The floor is lava. Do not run on the floor is lava. This includes yourself."

> Expense policy: meals are reimbursable. Meals that were a door are reimbursable with
> approval. Meals that were the chosen one are not reimbursable and we are not taking
> questions.

> From: Finance
> To: You
> Re: Q3 overrun
> The figure you have sent us does not match the figure in the system. Please reconcile.
> Thank you for your patience.
>
> — sent to you, internally, by Finance, in which there is one person, who is a
> lich, who has written this exact memo eleven times.

## 5b. THE DEMOTION MEMOS (locked decision 3)

Demotion is **announced, never experienced.** No scene, no conversation, no villain
explaining it. A memo is present in the morning and the title on it has changed. The
horror is that it was always going to happen and nothing in the job would have prevented
it. Each one removes a verb.

### First demotion — you lose the signature

> **Re:** Title change
> Effective immediately. Please update the letterhead. The letterhead is a courtesy.
>
> You will no longer be authorized to sign the hazard assessment. A Deputy will sign it.
> The Deputy has been informed. The Deputy does not have your years of context and has
> asked for none.
>
> — signed, *The Dark Lord*, and beneath it: *noted*

### Second demotion — you lose the escalation

> **Re:** Escalation rights
> A manager (Acting) may not refer a matter upward. This is not a demotion in your duties.
> It is a demotion in your *ability to tell anyone else about them.*
>
> Please continue to file matters as you see fit.

That second line — "file matters as you see fit" — is the meanest thing anyone has ever
said to you, and it is four words of bureaucratic indifference.

### Third demotion — the Mines

The ending. He does not threaten it. He has simply stopped needing a manager.

> **Re:** Assignment
> Following a review of regional performance across the period, you have been assigned to
> the Mines, effective today.
>
> Your access to the filing system will be retained. You will not require it.
>
> The Chosen One's next scheduled incursion is Tuesday. He has not been informed. He was
> never informed. There is no field on the form for informing him and there has never been
> any need for one.

**The final beat.** The Chosen One walks in on Tuesday, the fortress is unmanaged, the trap
is still compliant, and the game ends on the same note it has run on for a month: he is a
calendar problem, and nobody is holding the calendar.

> **INCIDENT REFERRAL — FORM 7B**
> Re: The chosen one
> Status: Requisitioned.
> Witness: Unassigned.
> Follow-up required: No. There is no follow-up. The trap is compliant.
>
> **Notes:** The trap was compliant before you arrived. The trap remained compliant. The
> trap will be compliant after this. No party is responsible for the trap, the calendar, or
> the outcome, and no party is available to be responsible.
>
> Filing closed.

## 5c. THE PROMOTION LADDER (design §7)

**The one rule:** the player must be *told they were wrong* before they can choose to be
wrong usefully. Verdicts are the only thing the game teaches. It never marks a misfiling
opportunity — that would be the game telling a joke instead of the player discovering one.

### The verdicts

> **Filing accepted.** The matter is closed. No follow-up required.

> **Filing rejected.** Correct category: Safety Compliance. This filing has been returned.
>
> You may refile. There is no deadline. There has never been a deadline.

> **Filing insufficient.** The filing was correct. It was also not enough. Both of these
> facts are noted and neither has changed the other.

The third verdict is the most important line in the game. It is a loss that the player
caused *by being right*, and it is the setup for the letterhead promotion.

### The catch-all misfiling — the memo that reads as a warning

Fired when the player files OTHER / UNCATEGORIZED on purpose. It is framed, in-world, as
punishment. It is a promotion. Neither is ever stated.

> **Re:** Filing quality
> Your filing is not compliant with the category definitions. This is the third such filing
> this quarter.
>
> Continue as you are.
>
> *There is no signature. There has never needed to be one. The letterhead is a courtesy.*

**The reveal beat — Career Position rises, and nobody explains why.** The narration reports
the title change in the same flat register it uses for everything. There is no flourish. The
player should have to work out on their own that being bad at the job is the only thing that
has ever worked for them.

> Your title has been updated. The effective date is today. No meeting has been scheduled and
> none is required.
>
> The filing quality concern raised on the 14th is considered closed.

*Considered closed.* The complaint was the mechanism. Nobody signed off on the complaint.

### The letterhead — promoted for a loss

> **Re:** Quarterly review
> Your filing on [matter] has been noted as correct.
>
> The outcome of that matter was not favourable to this office. You are not responsible for
> the outcome. You are, however, the only member of staff who was present for the paperwork.
>
> Your title has been restored. No further action is required of you.

**This is the one recovery that is technically deserved and completely hollow.** The player
did nothing right. They survived something unwinnable and were rewarded for the paperwork.

### Goblin advocacy — the reward for a quiet week

Fires on a day with no incursion, at Morale 9+. The goblins have used the leverage they have
been building since the union beat, and the Dark Lord concedes a title to prevent a slow
Tuesday. It is the only promotion caused by something kind.

> **Re:** Staffing — lower levels
> The lower levels have raised a matter regarding break entitlements. The matter is being
> reviewed on the basis of operational continuity.
>
> Your title has been restored as a result of the review. You were not party to the review.
> You were, however, the subject of a considerable amount of it.

> *Goblin 1:* We said we'd be reasonable.
> *Goblin 4:* We were reasonable. Reasonably, at volume, with a timeline.

**The goblins do not celebrate this.** They asked for break entitlements and got a manager's
title for someone else. They know exactly what they traded. Neither of them mentions it again.

### Probation — the cost of climbing

Every recovery sets Career Position to 5 and applies −1 per day for three days. There is no
memo for this. It appears as three unremarkable mornings where something goes slightly wrong
and no filing you make will stop it.

> Tuesday's filing has been returned.
>
> This is the second such return this month. The previous one is also being returned, for
> completeness, as it was outstanding.
>
> No reason has been provided for either.

### Hero defeated — the run ends, and the game does not celebrate

Three consecutive correct Chosen One filings across three Tuesdays. Never surfaced as a goal.
The win state is a quiet administrative fact, because if the game made it triumphant it would
admit the hero was the point.

> **Re:** Scheduled incursion — 14 March
> The scheduled incursion did not occur.
>
> The matter is closed. The calendar entry has been removed. No replacement has been
> scheduled, as the requirement has been met.
>
> Your title has been restored to Regional Manager. The office thanks you for the period of
> your service, which was, on balance, the entirety of the calendar.

## 5d. THINGS THE PROMOTION MUST NEVER SAY

The instant the game acknowledges that bad filing is rewarded, the joke is over and the
player is playing a score-chase with a paperwork skin.

- ✗ "Filing quality reduced. Career advancement possible." — the game explaining its own gag
- ✗ Any notification, badge, or "promotion available" prompt on a wrong filing
- ✗ A goblin saying "that was the wrong filing, obviously" — the goblins are not the
  tutorial, and they would not be smug, they would just be tired
- ✗ A scene where the player explains their promotion to anyone
- ✗ The Dark Lord acknowledging that incompetence is career-advantageous, in any form, ever

**The tell:** if the player can point at a screen and say *this is where the game told me*,
it was told too clearly. The finding-out has to be the player's.

## 6. LINES WE WILL REJECT

Keep this list visible. Most of these are fine jokes that break the game.

- ✗ "Welcome to the Dungeon, employee of the month!" — wink. Breaks rule 1.
- ✗ A leering villain explaining his evil plan — the Dark Lord never explains, he memoizes.
- ✗ A skeleton with a mug reading "World's Best Yordle" — cute, wrong tone, from a different game.
- ✗ The hero making a quip about "middle management energy" — the hero is sincere. He is
  slightly wrong. That is his function.
- ✗ Punching a goblin and the goblin says "I have a family" — the joke is at the *fantasy's*
  expense, and this is at a worker's expense. Cut it every time, without exception.
- ✗ Anything where the game acknowledges it is a joke about corporate culture.

The tell: if it would work in an office, it is too real. If it would work in a serious
fantasy, it is not doing enough. It has to fail both tests to stay.

This is the joke: you cannot deprecate destiny, and if you try, the paperwork increases.
